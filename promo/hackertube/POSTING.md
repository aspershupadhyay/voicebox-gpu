# Posting the HackerTube Reel

## Title (YouTube Short)
Karpathy rebuilt YouTube recommendations in 2 hours with Claude Code

## Caption (Instagram / YouTube description)
Andrej Karpathy got tired of YouTube's recommendations, so he had Claude read 43 million Hacker News comments from 20
years and keep only the 6,454 videos people recommend again and again.

The result is HackerTube (hackertube.org):
• no login: your history stays in your browser
• every thumbnail is a real frame from the middle of the video, not an A/B-tested upload
• all the data is one zip file: point your Claude at it and build your own

His only manual work: a Google Cloud API key, a server and the domain. Claude Code did the rest, about two hours with
Opus 5.5.

"What else are we not thinking of?"

Source: @karpathy on X, 1 Nov 2026.
