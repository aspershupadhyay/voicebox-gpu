# The planning sheet

Fill this before writing a single scene. The owner can read it in a minute and change what pops up and when: that's
the part Claude can't see on its own. Keep it in the project as `PLAN.md` and show it to the owner when they're
around; otherwise make the calls and note why.

## The header
- **What it's for** (one line): e.g. "a YouTube tutorial: 7 rules for making videos with Claude".
- **Who it's for**: e.g. "creators who've tried AI video and got slop".
- **Format**: landscape 1920×1080 (YouTube) or vertical 1080×1920 (Reels/Shorts), target length.
- **Look**: launch film or paper house (style-guide.md), the accent colour, the fonts.
- **Voice**: sparsh / harsh (`myvoice`), style (hype / tech), tempo.
- **Music**: which bed from the library, and where its energy should sit (intro quiet under the hook, breakdown
  under the slow part).

## One row per script line
| # | Line (as said) | Scene type | What appears, on which word | Sound | Caption? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| l01 | Everyone asks Claude for the prompt that makes viral videos. There isn't one. | captions + UI | a chat box already typing on frame 1; on *There* it deletes itself; "There isn't one." rises in serif | keys under the typing, a delete blip, the sub hit on *There* | no (on screen) |
| l06 | Ask for a cool motion video and you get slop… | half screen | left: the prompt → glossy thumbnails that grey out on *slop*; right: a director's notes, one per beat → a film strip on *film* | mouse clicks per note, a fail blip on *slop*, an airy pass under the strip | yes |
| l12 | Script on the left, the scene on the right… | full screen | the planning sheet itself; each scene-type chip lands on its word | card flips per row, a pop per chip | yes |

Scene types: **captions**, **captions + UI**, **half screen**, **full screen** (style-guide.md). Mix them: a full
screen after two caption lines, never four full screens in a row. Every section opens on its title card (the wipe,
the number) and the stepper moves on.

## Checks before building
- Does the hook land all three on frame 1: moving picture, text, voice (from ~0.05 s)?
- Is there something new every 2-3 s?
- Is every number sourced, and labelled as an estimate where it is one?
- Does the end hold still on an end card (no loop back to the first frame)?
